MY PERSPECTIVE

I'm interested in the space between what people want and why they want it.

Fashion, beauty, media, and retail are especially interesting to me because they sit at the intersection of identity and commerce. A product can be functional, but a brand can become part of how someone sees themselves.

My work combines the creative side of marketing with the analytical side: consumer psychology, cultural observation, research, strategy, and visual storytelling.

I’m particularly drawn to brands that create aspiration through inspiration rather than insecurity.
